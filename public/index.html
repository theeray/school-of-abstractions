Image status, authorship, and quotations

The painting shown here is an optimized temporary study image. The definitive Topaz web master is still pending approval. Deep zoom can enlarge this study image but cannot supply detail absent from its source.

The twenty figure and symbol labels follow Eric’s stated intentions. Positions are fitted to the study composition and can be recalibrated when the master changes. The painting includes allusions, not replicas guaranteed to follow every historical work’s original instructions.

Quoted dialogue is drawn from the artist-approved excerpts. New interpretive passages are not presented as recovered quotations. The active-work estimate is retrospective and excludes much of the reading and reflection between sessions.

The TAD logo is credited by Eric to Steve Sundahl. The Bauhaus and De Stijl comparisons remain interpretations until Sundahl confirms or rejects those influences. His quotation has not yet been supplied.
